Forgerie prototypes

Three screens, one language.

Built on the foundation from the finance prototypes. The verdict is a sentence, colour sits on the words carrying state, and hairlines do the containing.

Screens

Number
How many sessions do I have?
Twenty-five is a lie. Two need a decision. Tap the figure for the subtraction.
Pace
Are my agents going to be okay?
Context is the thing they run out of, so it takes the shape of a spend cap: fill, pace marker, verdict in the headline.
Drill
What did this one actually do?
The churn first, the files second, the transcript third. Ordered by what changed rather than by the clock.

Light mode is untested on a device. These inherit prefers-color-scheme from the finance foundation and were only captured dark. The finance screens read best light, so check these there before judging the palette.
