One rule covers all three voices, with no new data from the server. While the turn runs, every assistant text block is interim. When the turn closes, the last text block becomes the answer.

  • Two registers. Interim narration drops to the small muted voice, in place. The answer keeps the reading voice.
  • Process rail. Narration and step folds share one hairline rail, in the order they happened. The answer stands outside it.
  • Promoted answer. The work log stays quiet and unmarked. The answer alone carries an accent hairline and the turn meta.

The cost is the promotion flicker. The last interim block restyles the moment the turn completes, and on the rail variant it also moves outside the rail. If that motion reads as jumpy, the promoted-answer variant hides it best, because the block only brightens in place.
