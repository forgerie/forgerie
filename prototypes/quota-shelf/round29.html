The percentage is gone. A level tells you how much is left and leaves you to do the division; a duration answers the question you had. And once the readout is time, both risks become one comparison you can make without reading a bar: lasts longer than it takes to reset means credit will expire unspent, lasts less means it runs dry early. No history is needed for the rate — a window states its own length and its own reset, so the elapsed part is known and the share already spent over it is the rate.

Three readings of "remaining": reset drops the number entirely and keeps one countdown to the next loss. pin shows how long the pinned account lasts, beside when it resets. group shows how long the whole provider lasts if you are willing to re-pin, beside the next moment anything is lost.
