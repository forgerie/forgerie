Round 47 · width buys columns, height buys names

Round 46 found the shelf 12 px taller than the whole folded-landscape screen, 711 × 374, before a pixel of chrome. The cause is structural, not numeric: every rung since round 33 keys off width alone. Trimming 12 px would hide the fault — a panel with chrome gives the shelf about 280 px, not 374, so the real shortfall is closer to a hundred.

The lever is the account address. It is the only thing on the shelf that costs a whole line per account, and it answers no question the shelf exists to answer — it tells you which account, which matters when you go to act, not when you are deciding whether to act. Seven accounts is seven lines and the room around them, about 130 px. Nothing else is remotely as expensive.

At 711 × 374 width says yes to the address and height says no, and height wins. What never goes, at any size, is the lane, the pace cut, the hatch and the doom bar — the three questions the shelf was built to answer, drawn in geometry that costs 3 px a line.
