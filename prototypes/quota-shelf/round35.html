Round 35 · what a boolean should look like

Round 34's strip sits on the same rail as a meter, at the same height, in the same grey. On most rows that survives, because a meter carries a pace cut and a boolean does not. On Antigravity it does not. Antigravity reports no window length, so its metered lanes have no cut either and are drawn as plain bars — a flat bar above a flat strip, with nothing saying which is a quantity and which is a roster.

So the strip has to read as a different kind of thing, not just a different colour. Four questions decide it: with nothing blocked, is the row calm? With one of a hundred blocked, can you find it? With everything blocked, is it obvious? And could you mistake it for a meter?

bar is round 34. ghost drops available segments to the empty track, so only trouble is drawn. notch makes available accounts a 1 px line and blocked ones rise to 3 px. tick is one hairline for the whole roster with blocked accounts standing on it. count drops segments altogether for a single proportion — it scales without limit but names nobody.
