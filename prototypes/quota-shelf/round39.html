Round 39 · show the balance

Rounds 36 to 38 made the same mistake in different clothes. Having proved there is no scale to plot a balance on, each one went looking for a quieter mark to plot it with anyway — a shorter bar, a hairline, a dot, a dot with a wire behind it. All of them are the same error. A mark can only say “more” or “less”, and there is no “more than what” here.

The numbers were banned in round 30 for a specific reason: the geometry already carried the fact, so a number repeated it. That holds on a metered lane — a fill against a pace cut says everything a percentage would. It does not hold here. There is no fill, no cut and no window, so nothing is carrying the fact. The value is not a repeat of the drawing; it is the only content the lane has.

So the question is where the value sits, not what mark replaces it. origin puts it where every bar on the shelf begins. trail right-aligns it at the far end of the track. inline sets it hard against the tag. big is origin in the row's largest type. Rounding is the same in all four: cents below ten dollars, whole dollars above, because $47.31 is four characters of noise when the fact is “about fifty”.
