Quota shelf — four layouts

One fixture, one plan(width, height), four renderings, each executed across the same seven-rung size ladder. The two problems being solved: a shelf that does not state how much fuel is left, and an unmetered pool drawn as a full tank.

  • A · Ledger

    Today's shape, made honest. unmetered replaces the level, money replaces the share. Densest: all seven accounts fit a 620 px column.

  • B · Fuel

    One big number per account: the lane that binds. Surfaces the five-hour Claude window that today's shelf hides. Costs height — about four accounts per 620 px.

  • C · Runway

    Percentages become time, with a reset mark on each lane's timeline. Answers "can I finish this task". Costs height and needs history for a real pace on Antigravity.

  • D · Trend

    A 24-hour line instead of a level, and a tick per 429 for the unmetered lane. Needs a rolling sample buffer in the router.
