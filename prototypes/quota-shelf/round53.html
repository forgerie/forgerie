Round 53 · every account on one axis, and the loss added up

Round 28 said a pooled bar cannot work because its length is a mean. That was true of what it tested and wrong as a law, and this is where the law gets narrower: what cannot be averaged is position. Two things go on one line without averaging anything.

A barcode plot — every observation as a thin stripe on one shared axis, translucent so overlaps stack. Nothing is summarised; every mark is where it is. And a sum — credit that will be lost is a quantity, and quantities add. Round 19's starving account contributes zero to the group's loss; the wasting one contributes all of its surplus. Nothing cancels because nothing is divided.

The axis is the doom rate from round 33: fill ÷ pace, how many times the window's natural rate you would have to sustain to waste nothing. Unit-free, so a five-hour lane and a seven-day lane land on the same scale. Drawn diverging, on pace at the centre, on a symmetric log scale so 2× and ½× sit the same distance out; the edges are 8× and ⅛×. On the right, the loss: what share of the group's pooled allowance will go unspent if nothing changes, summed across every meter.
