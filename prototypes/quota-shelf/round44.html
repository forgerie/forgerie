Round 44 · the address is not a broken row

In round 43 the address is 10 px, in the same grey as the meter labels, left-aligned in the same slot they occupy, 2 px above the first of them. It shares every attribute with the rows beneath it, so it reads as one of them — specifically as a lane row whose bar failed to draw. That is the whole of the afterthought feeling, and it is not a matter of polish: a heading that uses none of a heading's tools is not a heading.

A heading has exactly three tools — size and weight, colour, and position. Round 43 uses none of them. Each candidate below adds one.
