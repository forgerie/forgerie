Round 43 · the address above the lanes it names

Round 42 put the account address in a fixed 132 px column on the left. That column bought perfect alignment and charged two things for it: the address truncated to sjackson@amazeinsig… even at 980 px wide, and every lane on the shelf gave up 132 px of track to hold a label that only changes once per account.

Above the lanes, both charges reverse. The lanes get the whole width back, the address is never cut, and an account's identity sits directly over that account's bars instead of beside them. It also makes the grouping self-evident — a heading separates accounts better than a 5 px gap, so the gap goes.

What it costs is a line of height per account, which is the one thing this shelf has spent forty rounds refusing to spend. Both layouts are built below from the same live data, so the trade is measured rather than argued.
