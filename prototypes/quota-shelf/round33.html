Round 33 · solid doom, across three different clocks

Round 32 chose solid on five Codex accounts, and leaned on something Codex happens to have: one seven-day window shared by every account, so the pace cut is a clock and time left maps onto a fixed horizon.

Claude breaks that inside one row. Each account carries a five-hour meter, a seven-day meter and a Fable meter, so three window lengths stack together. A three-day horizon glows on every five-hour lane for its whole life — five hours is always “soon” against three days — and the ramp becomes a light that never goes out.

So stop measuring the deadline and measure whether the credit can still be spent. To waste nothing you must burn the surplus in the time left, at surplus / timeLeft, against the window’s own natural rate of 100 / window. That ratio reduces to fill ÷ pace: how many times the natural rate you would have to sustain. Unit-free, identical in meaning on a five-hour lane and a seven-day one, and made of the two numbers the lane already draws.
