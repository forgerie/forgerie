Round 25 · both risks, with three accounts on different days

Round 24's draining walk held a single account, which is not the case that matters. Here every group has three accounts resetting on three different days, none of them failing over, because every provider is set to pinned routing. Each row is drawn twice: reach names the fullest usable account, pinned names the account the router is held to and counts down to the next thing the group loses.

1 · Losing credit in waves

Three accounts hold 40 %, 80 % and 95 %, resetting in 6 d, 4 d and 2 d. Nothing is spent; only the clock moves. Each account's cut crosses its own fill at its own moment, so the group's hollow grows in steps.

2 · What a single bar costs

One account about to lose 89 points, in groups of one, two, three and six. The others sit exactly on pace and contribute nothing. The pooled fill is a mean, so the loss is divided by the account count — this is the price of one bar, in points.

3 · Spending too fast, across accounts

The first four steps drain all three together. The last two drain only the pinned account while the spares sit full — which switches the hatch gate off, and leaves the readout to carry the warning alone.
