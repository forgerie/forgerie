Round 40 · the boolean belongs to its account

Round 34 pulled every Gemini boolean out of its account and collected them into one strip at the foot of the Antigravity row. That fixed the alignment problem it was built for and created a worse one: the row shows two account blocks, and then two tokens belonging to nobody. The first is account one's and the second is account two's, and nothing in the drawing says so — you have to know the order.

One rule covers both shapes the shelf has to hold. An account with meters keeps its token in its own block, beside its lanes. An account that is only a boolean is nothing but a token, so many of those share one line as a roster. That second case is what the hundred-account test was really about: a hundred boolean-only accounts are a hundred tokens on one line, while a hundred metered accounts are a hundred blocks and no boolean treatment can help with that.

apart is round 34's strip. below gives the token its own line at the foot of its block. gutter puts it in the block's left margin. tail puts it at the right edge, in the same gutter OpenRouter's balance now uses — lanes grow from the left, and whatever cannot be drawn as a lane sits at the right.
