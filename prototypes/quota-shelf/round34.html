Round 34 · solid doom, grouped accounts, and a strip that scales

1 · The doomed credit stays solid

Round 33 ramped the bar from an outline up to a filled block, so a real loss three days out was drawn as an empty box. The bar is now solid at every distance and the intensity moves to hue: amber #d29922 through red #f85149 as the rate you would have to sustain to save the credit climbs. Amber and red stay the only saturated marks, and both still mean a problem.

2 · Accounts are grouped, where grouping means something

Claude stacks three meters per account, so six lanes were one undifferentiated block and the gap tells you which three belong together. Codex holds one meter per account, so there is nothing to group — the lane is the account, and a gap there only spends height. A row is separated when any of its accounts carries more than one lane, and otherwise its lanes sit at the plain 4 px pitch. The boolean strip is always held apart, being a different kind of thing from the account above it.

3 · The lanes start at the same x on every row

Antigravity's Gemini dots used to sit before the stack and push its bars right, so no row lined up with any other. The booleans are now a lane: one strip across the same track, one segment per account, red where blocked. Segments are flex: 1, so the strip is the full track at any count and the row never grows sideways.
