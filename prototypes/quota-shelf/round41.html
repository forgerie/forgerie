What is in this drawing

  • Fill against the pace cut — an even spend from here either leaves credit over or runs out early.
  • Amber-to-red solid bar — credit that will not be spent in time, hue by fill ÷ pace: how many times the window's own natural rate you would have to sustain to waste nothing.
  • Hatch — spending behind the even rate, so the lane runs dry before its reset.
  • Riser — the pinned account.
  • Token — a boolean, under the account that owns it. Red when it cannot serve.
  • Number — a balance, because it has no scale to be plotted on.
