Round 20 · a single bar that does not cancel

Pool failed on arithmetic, not on the idea. It averaged a signed overshoot, so one account being 70 points behind erased another being 70 ahead. Waste does not work that way — one account running short does not un-waste another — so the sum takes positive terms only: at risk = mean of max(0, fill − pace). That quantity can never cancel, and it is one number, so it can ride a single bar.
