Nothing about the idea needs any of that. The axis is a rail — the same 3 px ground every lane already sits on — with a 1 px tick at centre for on-pace. Every meter is a short stripe standing on the rail at its rate. The loss is a number in the countdown column, with the arrow the countdown already uses.
