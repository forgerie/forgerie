Put type, space, and radius on shared scales. Text sizes move onto a fluid
four-step ramp that breathes with the surface that holds it, corners collapse
from nine ad-hoc radii onto a three-tier scale (inline, control, surface) plus
the pill, and named fluid space tokens debut on the settings cards. Control
text stays at a static 16px so mobile browsers do not zoom focused inputs.
