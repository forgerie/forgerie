Remove the red bar above the chat. The status chip in the session header is now the one place that says something is wrong: "Reconnecting", "Disconnected", or "Problem". When it has more to say, the chip is a button that opens a panel. While reconnecting, the panel shows why the connection dropped, which try is next and when it starts, and a Try now button. Each failure stays in the panel with its time and full text until you dismiss it, and you can copy it. During a run, the chip keeps "Running" and shows a count of problems. A message that was refused before it was sent shows as a short note above the message box, next to the text that came back.
