Show Decision hints in the interface. The session list marks a session that a hint moved to Needs your response with a hollow square and a short word for what the reply seems to be. Gate cards and bash lines show a risk label (Low risk, Check first or High risk) with the raised signals and the answering model; it is an opinion, never an approval, and its late arrival does not move the approve and refuse buttons. Settings > Plugins > Decision hints shows whether sessiond has a gateway key, never the key. With the plugin off, nothing changes.
