Search, copy, and live-branch-first ordering in the /tree navigator. Typing in the search field narrows the history to entries matching every term across their label, kind and text, and an empty result says it was the search rather than the filter. Copy puts the selected entry's text on the clipboard from the control row, so rows keep no per-row chrome. At a fork, the branch you are actually on now reads before abandoned siblings instead of following transport order.
