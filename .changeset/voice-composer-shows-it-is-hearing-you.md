Show the voice composer hearing you, instead of only claiming to listen.

The status dot is grey and pulsing while the microphone is open and waiting, and turns accent-coloured while a voice is actually arriving. A clock beside it counts the speech captured so far. Both are driven by the voiced-audio reports the capture session already emits, so they are evidence rather than decoration.

There is no amplitude anywhere in this pipeline, so nothing here draws a waveform or a level meter. A meter invented from sample counts would look confident and say nothing true about how loud you are.
