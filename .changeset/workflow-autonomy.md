Add Settings > Plugins > Workflow autonomy. By default, Forgerie lands a workflow's branches itself at a land stop, and a conflict still waits for you. You can also let it pass approve stops. Each reviewer gets at most two review rounds per run by default; after that, the agent files the remaining findings as issues. Forgerie's decisions show in the transcript, the plan and the decision log.
