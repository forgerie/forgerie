Rebuild the composer as one floating island and retire the bottom status bar.

The composer is now an inset surface with a single control rail: send becomes queue while the agent works, steering joins the primary control, model and thinking share one capsule, and attachments and the session controls share one menu that also counts what is waiting. Context use is drawn on the island's own edge. The status bar's readings — tokens, cost, queued messages, warnings — moved into the sheet that menu opens, so the transcript keeps the height the bar used to hold. Session identity and archive now appear when the composer itself has room, and the composer sizes from its own container rather than the viewport.
