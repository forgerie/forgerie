Replies can show seven more blocks: `checks` for test and verification results, `findings` for review defects (worst first, with locations that open the file), `recap` for where work stands with what needs you first, `verdict` for a yes, no or partly answer, `commits` for revisions and how far each got, `delta` for before and after numbers read as better or worse, and `ledger` for money with totals and signed negatives. A `files` block with no marks now shows a layout with aligned notes, and `claims` takes the research verdicts supported, partly, unsupported and contradicted. Agents learn the new blocks from the reply guide, which grows to about 3,600 characters.
