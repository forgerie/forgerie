Give the radial menu one bearing, dictation, and open a keyboard-free composer on release.

Holding the transcript and flicking west now opens a dialog that is already listening. A finished sentence counts down for two seconds in plain sight and sends itself; touching the text stops the countdown and keeps every word. The keyboard appears only when a thumb taps the text to fix something.

The wheel keeps all eight bearings so the gesture never changes shape, but the other seven are held empty for now. The previous panel assignment stays in `localStorage` under the v1 key, so restoring Inbox, Files, Git, Terminal, Notifications and More is a data change rather than a rebuild.
