Move a turn's time and model onto the line that closes it, opposite the step count, instead of printing them above every reply. The row was near-identical on each message of a turn — same model, same effort, timestamps seconds apart — so repeating it added noise between every pair of paragraphs without telling you anything new. Now one quiet line ends each turn: how many steps it took on the left, when it finished and what produced it on the right.
