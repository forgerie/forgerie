The composer now shows lists and quotes the way the chat shows them. A `-`, `*`, or `+` item shows a bullet, a numbered item shows its number, and long items wrap under their own text. A `>` line shows a quote bar with muted text, and a nested quote shows one bar for each level. The message you send is still the same Markdown. Backspace at the start of an item removes its bullet, or moves a nested item out one level. Home goes to the start of the item's text.
