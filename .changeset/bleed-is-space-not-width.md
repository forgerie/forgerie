Give the transcript one column. Prose, cards, the activity fold, and the
composer share both edges of the reading measure; content that is not prose —
a table, a code block — keeps the column's start edge and runs past its end
into the free space beside it, taking only as much as its content asks for. A
narrow table now produces no ragged edge at all, and the composer matches the
prose block it answers instead of the widest table on the page.
