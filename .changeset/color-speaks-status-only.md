Make color mean something. Panel-tab count badges drop their green success
dress for neutral chrome (the unread variant keeps its accent), skill
disclosures use the interactive accent instead of decorative purple, every
focus indicator standardizes on the same two-pixel accent ring, and the
light palette's muted and dim inks darken a step to clear WCAG AA contrast.
Terminal fallback colors align with the current palette.
