The composer now shows more Markdown as you type.

- A task item (`- [ ] `) shows a checkbox. Tap it, or press Ctrl+Shift+X, to check or clear it. Enter continues the list with an unchecked item.
- Bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, and link text show their style, and their markers show in a muted color.
- A leading `/command` shows as a chip.
- Pasting a URL over selected text makes that text a link.
- Spelling, autocorrect, and capitals turn off while the caret is in an `@file`, `#model`, or `/command` token.
- Lists and quotes in right-to-left text indent and draw their bars from the right.
