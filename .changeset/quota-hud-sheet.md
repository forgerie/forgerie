The account meters are now a compact heads-up display: they draw no account addresses at any size. Press an account to open the Quota sheet at it, with every account's address, every window (including those turned off in Settings), the reset chips, and a refresh. An Antigravity pool is drawn as a run of dots across its lane, and an OpenRouter balance as one pip per dollar with its amount beside the shares and no clock.
