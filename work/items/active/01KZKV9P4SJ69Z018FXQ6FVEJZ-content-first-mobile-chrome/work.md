# Content-first mobile chat chrome

On constrained panes, resident chrome above the transcript goes to zero:
liveness moves into the transcript, identity and the summon control move into
the composer row, and everything else becomes a bottom sheet on request.
Design record and rejected alternatives: `prototypes/mobile-chrome/README.md`.
